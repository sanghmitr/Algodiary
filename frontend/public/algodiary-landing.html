How it works

Simple by design, serious by purpose

01
Set your target companies and date
Tell AlgoDiary which companies you're targeting and your goal date. The 90-day plan seeds automatically — all 90 days of DSA, HLD, and LLD tasks ready on day one.
02
Work through daily tasks, take notes as you go
Each day has 3 parallel streams. Solve the LeetCode problem, study the HLD system, implement the LLD pattern. Write notes and snippets linked directly to each task.
03
Search your knowledge base before interviews
Days before the interview, open the Knowledge Base. Search "sliding window" or "rate limiter" — every note and snippet you ever wrote surfaces instantly. Revision in seconds.
04
Track mocks, log weak areas, iterate
Log mock interview scores, track weak areas by category, and watch your DSA/HLD/LLD curves trend upward. The data tells you where to focus your next session.
